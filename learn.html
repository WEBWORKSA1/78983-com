The 5-number money checkup

Your savings rate, emergency-fund months, debt-to-income, net worth trend and retirement gap. If you know these five numbers, you know where you stand. Start with the savings goal and debt payoff tools.

Rule of 72, explained

Divide 72 by an annual growth rate to estimate the years it takes to double: 72 ÷ 8% ≈ 9 years. The same trick works for inflation — at 3%, prices double in about 24 years.

Why APR beats the headline rate

APR folds in many fees and gives a truer cost of borrowing. Two loans with the same rate can have very different APRs.

AI costs for small businesses

Token pricing scales with usage. Measure average prompt and reply sizes, cache what repeats and route easy tasks to smaller models. Our AI cost estimator models it.
