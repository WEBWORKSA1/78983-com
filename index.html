Frequently asked questions

Is 78983 really free?

Yes. All calculators are free with no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising, voluntary support and sponsorships.

Do you store what I type into calculators?

No. Calculations run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter into a calculator is sent to us.

Is this financial advice?

No. Our tools and guides are educational. For decisions specific to you, use our free review to connect with a qualified professional.

What does the name 78983 mean?

Read it as 7-8-9, then 83: three steps climbing, then a finish. The digits also add up to 35, whose digital root is 8 — a numeral associated with prosperity in Chinese culture. We think it's a fitting name for tools that help numbers grow.
