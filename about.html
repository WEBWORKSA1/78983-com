About 78983

Free, private calculators and plain-English guides for the numbers behind life's biggest decisions.

Our mission

Big decisions — a home, a loan, a business, a retirement plan, an AI rollout — come down to numbers. Most people never see the math. 78983 exists to make that math visible, free and private, with tools that update as you type and guides that explain every formula in plain English.

What makes us different

  • Private by design: calculations run in your browser; nothing you type into a calculator is uploaded.
  • Show the work: every calculator includes its formula, assumptions, a worked example and an FAQ.
  • No paywalls, no sign-ups: the site is funded by clearly separated ads, voluntary support and labeled sponsorships.
  • Global: switch between six currencies; the math works everywhere.

Editorial standards

Calculator logic is tested against standard financial formulas, reviewed regularly and dated. When we use outside sources, we link to them. Advertisers and sponsors never influence calculator output or editorial content. Found an error? Tell us — we fix verified issues quickly and credit the reporter.

The name

Read it as 7-8-9, then 83: three steps climbing, then a finish line. The digits add up to 35 with a digital root of 8 — a numeral that represents prosperity in Chinese culture. It's a reminder of what good numbers can do when you understand them.

Important

78983 provides educational tools and general information, not personalized financial, legal, tax or investment advice. Consult a qualified professional before acting.
